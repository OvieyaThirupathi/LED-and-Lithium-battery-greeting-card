 **LED AND LITHIUM BATTERY GREETING CARD**  
   
**1\. Aim**  
To design and make a simple LED greeting card using a lithium battery, LED, and aluminium foil, demonstrating how an electrical circuit can be integrated into a creative greeting card.

**2\. Problem Statement**  
Traditional greeting cards are usually static and do not provide an interactive experience.  
To make the greeting card more attractive and creative, an LED lighting circuit can be incorporated into the card.  
The project uses a small battery as the power source and aluminium foil as a conducting path to illuminate the LED.

**3\. Proposed Solution**  
The proposed system is a light-up greeting card in which an LED is embedded inside the decorative flower arrangement.  
A small lithium battery supplies electrical energy to the LED. Aluminium foil is used to create conducting paths between the battery and LED.  
When the circuit is completed, current flows through the LED and it glows, making the greeting card attractive and interactive.

**4\. Components Required**  
💡 LED  
🔋 Small lithium/coin-cell battery  
Aluminium foil  
📄 A4 sheet / greeting card sheet  
✂️ Scissors  
🧴 Glue  
Jumper/connecting wires if required  
Decorative paper/flowers

**5\. Circuit Working**  
The lithium battery acts as the power source for the circuit.  
Aluminium foil is used as a conducting material to connect the battery to the LED.  
When the conducting paths are properly connected:  
Battery → Aluminium foil → LED → Aluminium foil → Battery  
Current flows through the LED and it produces light.  
When the connection is broken, current stops flowing and the LED turns OFF.  
Thus, the electrical circuit is used to create a simple light-up greeting card.

**6\. Procedure**  
Prepare the greeting card using an A4 sheet.  
Arrange and attach the decorative flowers on the card.  
Place the LED in the required position inside the flower arrangement.  
Place the small lithium battery at the back/inside portion of the card.  
Cut suitable strips of aluminium foil.  
Use the aluminium foil strips as conducting paths between the battery and LED.  
Connect the LED with the correct polarity.  
Check the connections carefully.  
Complete the circuit using the aluminium foil.  
When the circuit is completed, observe the LED glowing.
